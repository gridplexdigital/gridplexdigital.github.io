# Credits and editorial policy

**Series:** 123 Lenses: A Field Guide to People, Systems, and Ideas  
**Author and curator:** Dr. Aniket Pingley

The lesson notes are original introductory explanations written for this series. Each page includes suggested further reading and source credits. Those works are intellectual resources for further study, not quotations or substitutes for the original material.

When adding direct quotations, use only what is necessary, identify the author and work, and provide a reliable citation. Prefer original research, books, institutional reports and primary sources. Do not use unlicensed illustrations, photographs, charts or book-cover images.

A suggested licence for original site text is CC BY 4.0, subject to the author's confirmation. Third-party material remains under its own licence or copyright.

The idea

Institutions are the formal and informal rules that shape behaviour. Their quality depends on legitimacy, capacity, accountability and how rules are enforced. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Look beyond written policy. Ask who can enforce a rule, who benefits from it and whether people expect it to apply fairly.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which institution in your daily life works because people trust its rules?

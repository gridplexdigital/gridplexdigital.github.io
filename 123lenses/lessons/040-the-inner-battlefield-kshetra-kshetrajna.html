The inner battlefield: awareness versus the field of habit

The true Kurukshetra is not an ancient geographical plain; it is the human psyche, where the silent witness of awareness observes the field of conditioned impulses.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

The Gita establishes the distinction between Kshetra (the Field: our physical body, emotions, memories, and social conditioning) and Kshetrajna (the Knower of the Field: the conscious awareness that observes thoughts). Suffering occurs when the Knower identifies with every fleeting thought, rage, or craving arising in the Field.

Put it to use

Practice stepping back into the observer role. Say: 'Anger has arisen in the field,' rather than 'I am angry.'

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Can you observe your next bout of anxiety as a passing weather pattern in the field rather than your core identity?

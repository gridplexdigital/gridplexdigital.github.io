The idea

Justice concerns how benefits, burdens, rights, opportunities and recognition should be distributed. Different theories prioritise equality, liberty, need, desert or capability. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When judging a policy, identify the principle of fairness being used and who may be disadvantaged by it.

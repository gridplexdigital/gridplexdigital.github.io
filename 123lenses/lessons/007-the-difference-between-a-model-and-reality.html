The difference between a model and reality

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Models simplify reality to help explain or predict selected features. A useful model is not a complete picture; its assumptions define where it may fail. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Ask what a model leaves out and whether those omissions matter for the decision at hand.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which model or framework do you rely on beyond its useful range?

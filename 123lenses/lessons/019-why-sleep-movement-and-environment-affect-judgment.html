Why sleep, movement and environment affect judgment

Cognitive performance is shaped by basic conditions such as sleep, stress, movement and the environment. Good reasoning is harder when attention and energy are depleted.

The idea

Cognitive performance is shaped by basic conditions such as sleep, stress, movement and the environment. Good reasoning is harder when attention and energy are depleted. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Before interpreting a run of poor decisions as a character flaw, check sleep, workload and physical conditions. Seek professional advice for persistent health concerns.

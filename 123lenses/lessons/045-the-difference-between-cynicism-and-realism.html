The difference between cynicism and realism

Realism attends to evidence, incentives and constraints. Cynicism assumes bad motives too readily and can miss genuine generosity, competence and cooperation.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Realism attends to evidence, incentives and constraints. Cynicism assumes bad motives too readily and can miss genuine generosity, competence and cooperation. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Test both optimistic and suspicious explanations. Look for behaviour over time rather than relying on a single impression.

Correlation is not causation

Two things moving together does not establish that one caused the other. A third factor, reverse causation or coincidence may explain the pattern.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Two things moving together does not establish that one caused the other. A third factor, reverse causation or coincidence may explain the pattern. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When you see a relationship, sketch alternative causal explanations before choosing one.

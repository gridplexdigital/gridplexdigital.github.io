The three universal unifiers of humankind

Global cooperation across cultural divides was forged through three practical institutions: the monetary order, the imperial order, and universal religions.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Throughout history, humans instinctively divided the world into 'us' and 'them.' Yet three distinct forces overcame tribal suspicion: money (the supreme system of mutual trust, requiring no shared god), empires (which dissolved regional borders and standardized legal codes), and universal religions (which posited a single moral order binding all humans).

Put it to use

In diverse, cross-border collaborations, build common ground using pragmatic systems of mutual exchange before trying to resolve philosophical disagreements.

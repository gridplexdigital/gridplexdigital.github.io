Nyaya: knowledge needs reliable means

The Nyaya tradition analyses valid knowledge and discusses perception, inference, comparison and testimony. It offers a rigorous vocabulary for examining how claims are justified.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

The Nyaya tradition analyses valid knowledge and discusses perception, inference, comparison and testimony. It offers a rigorous vocabulary for examining how claims are justified. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When someone makes a claim, ask whether it comes from direct observation, inference, analogy or testimony—and whether that source is reliable here.

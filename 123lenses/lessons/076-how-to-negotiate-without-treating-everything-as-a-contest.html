How to negotiate without treating everything as a contest

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Good negotiation explores interests, alternatives and trade-offs. Positions are what people demand; interests are the reasons those demands matter. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Before a negotiation, identify your best alternative, your priorities and what the other side may value. Look for exchanges that cost you little but matter to them.

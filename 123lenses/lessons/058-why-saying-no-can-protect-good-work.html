Why saying no can protect good work

Every commitment uses time, attention and credibility. Saying yes without capacity can reduce the quality of existing obligations and create avoidable disappointment.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Every commitment uses time, attention and credibility. Saying yes without capacity can reduce the quality of existing obligations and create avoidable disappointment. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Make trade-offs explicit. A useful no can include what you can do, when you can do it or what would need to move.

The Cognitive Revolution and myth-making

The evolutionary breakthrough of Homo sapiens was not superior physical strength, but language capable of transmitting information about things that do not exist.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Most animals communicate danger or immediate location ('Careful, a lion!'). Homo sapiens developed the capacity to say 'The guardian spirit of the lion will protect our tribe if we unite.' This capacity for shared myth-making allowed humans to cooperate flexibly in groups of hundreds of thousands, breaking past Dunbar's biological limit of 150 individuals.

Put it to use

Mobilize teams and communities not merely through dry metrics, but by articulating a compelling, ethical shared narrative.

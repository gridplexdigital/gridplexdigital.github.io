Why cities create opportunity—and inequality

Cities concentrate people, skills, services and ideas, enabling exchange and innovation. They also concentrate congestion, pollution, exclusion and unequal access to opportunity.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Cities concentrate people, skills, services and ideas, enabling exchange and innovation. They also concentrate congestion, pollution, exclusion and unequal access to opportunity. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Examine a city through mobility, housing, public space, jobs and access to services—not just its skyline or economic output.

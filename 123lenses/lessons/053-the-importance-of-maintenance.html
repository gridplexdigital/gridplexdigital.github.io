The importance of maintenance

Systems often receive attention when they are launched or broken, while routine maintenance remains invisible. Neglected maintenance creates future cost and fragility.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Systems often receive attention when they are launched or broken, while routine maintenance remains invisible. Neglected maintenance creates future cost and fragility. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Include upkeep, documentation, ownership and replacement in every project plan. Treat maintenance as part of delivery, not an afterthought.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What valuable system around you is being maintained poorly?

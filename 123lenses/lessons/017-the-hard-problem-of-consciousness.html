The hard problem of consciousness

The idea

The hard problem asks why and how physical processes are accompanied by subjective experience. It is a live philosophical debate, not a settled proof for or against any metaphysical position. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Separate explanations of cognitive functions from explanations of subjective experience. Be precise about what a theory explains and what it leaves open.

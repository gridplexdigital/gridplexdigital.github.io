The difference between persuasion and manipulation

Persuasion offers reasons that can be examined. Manipulation often hides relevant information, exploits vulnerabilities or makes refusal unnecessarily difficult.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Persuasion offers reasons that can be examined. Manipulation often hides relevant information, exploits vulnerabilities or makes refusal unnecessarily difficult. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Ask whether the other person is free to disagree, has access to material facts and understands the trade-offs.

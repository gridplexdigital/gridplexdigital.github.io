Reciprocity: the social force of giving

People often feel pressure to return favours. Reciprocity supports cooperation, but it can also be used to create obligation or manipulate decisions.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

People often feel pressure to return favours. Reciprocity supports cooperation, but it can also be used to create obligation or manipulate decisions. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Accept generosity without automatically surrendering judgment. Be clear about whether a favour came with expectations.

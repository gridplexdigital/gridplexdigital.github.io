The idea

Evidence quality depends on relevance, measurement, comparison, sample selection, uncertainty and the possibility of alternative explanations. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Before sharing a claim, trace it to the original source and inspect how the data were collected. Headlines are not methods sections.

Path dependence: why history keeps shaping the present

Earlier choices can shape later options through infrastructure, habits, standards and institutions. A system may persist even when alternatives appear better.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Earlier choices can shape later options through infrastructure, habits, standards and institutions. A system may persist even when alternatives appear better. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When a process seems irrational, investigate how it developed and what switching costs keep it in place.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which current arrangement is easier to explain through its history than its present logic?

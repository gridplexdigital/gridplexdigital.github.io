The scientific revolution: admitting ignorance

Modern science began not with the discovery of great truths, but with the radical, humble willingness to say: 'We do not know.'

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Pre-modern religious and philosophical traditions asserted that all important knowledge was already revealed in sacred scriptures. The Scientific Revolution began with the Latin confession 'Ignoramus'—the revolutionary admission that humans do not know the answers to our most critical questions, making empirical observation and exploration imperative.

Put it to use

Cultivate the courage to say 'I don't know' in high-stakes meetings. It is the necessary prerequisite for genuine investigation.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What corporate or personal dogma are you defending as settled truth that would benefit from admitting ignorance?

Scepticism: how much can we really know?

Philosophical scepticism tests the grounds of belief. It can expose unjustified certainty, but radical doubt can also make ordinary action impossible.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Philosophical scepticism tests the grounds of belief. It can expose unjustified certainty, but radical doubt can also make ordinary action impossible. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Use proportionate confidence: demand stronger evidence for higher-stakes claims, while allowing practical decisions under uncertainty.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which of your beliefs rests on evidence, and which rests mainly on trust or habit?

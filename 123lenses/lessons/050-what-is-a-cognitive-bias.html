What is a cognitive bias?

The idea

A cognitive bias is a systematic pattern in judgment that can arise from shortcuts, context, motivation or the limits of attention. It is not a diagnosis of foolishness. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Learn biases as warning signs, not labels to attach to other people. Ask whether the mechanism plausibly applies in this situation.

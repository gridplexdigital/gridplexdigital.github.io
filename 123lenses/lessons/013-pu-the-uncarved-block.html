Pu: the power of the uncarved block

In simplicity and raw, unconditioned openness lies infinite potential. Returning to the original state shields against artificial dogma.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Pu (the uncarved block of wood) represents things in their natural, unshaped state—free from artificial categorization, social pretension, and carved dogmas. When wood is carved, it becomes a utensil, useful for one narrow task but dead to all others. The wise leader preserves the uncarved simplicity of mind that retains all possibilities.

Put it to use

Approach complex negotiations and design problems with the radical simplicity of a child before specialized jargon complicates the issue.

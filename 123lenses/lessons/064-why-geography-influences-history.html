The idea

Climate, terrain, rivers, disease environments and access to trade routes shape opportunities and constraints. They influence history without determining it completely. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

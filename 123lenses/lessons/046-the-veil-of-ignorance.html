The veil of ignorance

John Rawls asks us to imagine choosing principles for society without knowing our future position in it. The thought experiment tests whether rules are fair beyond self-interest.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

John Rawls asks us to imagine choosing principles for society without knowing our future position in it. The thought experiment tests whether rules are fair beyond self-interest. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When designing a rule, imagine you might occupy the least advantaged position affected by it.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Would you accept the rule if you did not know where you would end up?

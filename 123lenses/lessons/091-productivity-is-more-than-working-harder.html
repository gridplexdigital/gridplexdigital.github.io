Productivity is more than working harder

Productivity concerns output relative to inputs. It can improve through skills, tools, technology, better processes and coordination—not simply longer hours.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Productivity concerns output relative to inputs. It can improve through skills, tools, technology, better processes and coordination—not simply longer hours. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Look for recurring friction, rework and waiting. A small process improvement may create more value than adding effort.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What repeated task could be redesigned rather than done faster?

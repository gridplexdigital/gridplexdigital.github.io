Nishkama Karma: action detached from outcome

You have a right to your labor, but never to the fruits of your labor. Pour complete excellence into action while surrendering anxiety over results.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

When we act solely for the anticipated prize—wealth, praise, promotion, victory—our minds are divided between the craft and the scoreboard, inducing crippling anxiety and moral fragility. Nishkama Karma directs 100 percent of cognitive energy into the integrity of the action itself, treating the external outcome with serene equanimity.

Put it to use

Focus intensely on process and preparation. Treat the score as an external consequence beyond your absolute control.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What creative or professional project would you pursue with total joy if guaranteed zero public applause?

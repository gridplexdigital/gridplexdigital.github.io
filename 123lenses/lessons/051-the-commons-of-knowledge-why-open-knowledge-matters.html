The commons of knowledge: why open knowledge matters

Shared knowledge can be reused and improved by many people. Open access can widen participation, but quality, maintenance, attribution and sustainability still need attention.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Shared knowledge can be reused and improved by many people. Open access can widen participation, but quality, maintenance, attribution and sustainability still need attention. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When publishing knowledge, provide sources, clear licensing and a way for others to correct or build on it.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What knowledge could you make more useful by documenting it openly?

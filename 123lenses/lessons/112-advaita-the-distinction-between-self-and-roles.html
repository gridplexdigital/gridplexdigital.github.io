Advaita: the distinction between self and roles

The idea

Advaita Vedanta investigates the relationship between the individual self, awareness and ultimate reality. Its claims are philosophical and contemplative, not interchangeable with findings from neuroscience. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Examine the roles and descriptions you identify with. Ask what is observed, what is changing and what the tradition means by the witnessing self.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

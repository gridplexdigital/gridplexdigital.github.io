The difference between efficiency and effectiveness

Efficiency concerns the resources used relative to output. Effectiveness concerns whether the right outcome is achieved. A process can be efficient at doing the wrong thing.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Efficiency concerns the resources used relative to output. Effectiveness concerns whether the right outcome is achieved. A process can be efficient at doing the wrong thing. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Start with the outcome that matters, then assess the process. Do not celebrate speed or low cost without checking results.

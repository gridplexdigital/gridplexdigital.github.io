The idea

Science is a disciplined process of forming testable explanations, gathering evidence, exposing claims to criticism and revising conclusions. It is not a guarantee that every scientist is unbiased. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Turn strong opinions into testable predictions. Separate observation from interpretation and welcome attempts to disconfirm your explanation.

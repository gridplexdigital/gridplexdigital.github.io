The difference between kindness and people-pleasing

Kindness considers another person's welfare while retaining honesty and self-respect. People-pleasing may prioritise approval at the expense of truth or sustainable commitments.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Kindness considers another person's welfare while retaining honesty and self-respect. People-pleasing may prioritise approval at the expense of truth or sustainable commitments. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Offer help you can genuinely provide. Be honest about limits rather than making promises to avoid temporary discomfort.

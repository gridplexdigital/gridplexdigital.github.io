The fundamental attribution error

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

We can over-explain other people's behaviour through character while underestimating the situation they are in. Our own behaviour often receives more contextual explanation. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Before judging a person, generate at least one plausible situational explanation and check whether it fits the facts.

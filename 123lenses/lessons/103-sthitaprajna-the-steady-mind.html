Sthitaprajna: cultivating the unshakeable mind

The steady mind neither rejoices in prosperity nor collapses in adversity. Emotional equilibrium is the bedrock of wise judgment.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

The Sthitaprajna (one of stable wisdom) is like an ocean: rivers of praise and torrents of criticism pour continuously into it, yet its depth remains tranquil and unmoved. They draw their senses inward from distractions, like a tortoise retracting its limbs into its shell, remaining anchored in core purpose.

Put it to use

When praised or blamed, take three deep breaths before reacting. Do not let external emotional turbulence dictate your internal state.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

How quickly do compliments inflate your ego, or criticisms paralyze your confidence?

Why measurement changes behaviour

The idea

When a measure becomes a target, people may optimise the number rather than the underlying goal. Metrics are useful signals, but they can distort behaviour when treated as the whole truth. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Pair outcome metrics with quality checks and qualitative evidence. Ask what people might do to improve the score without improving the service.

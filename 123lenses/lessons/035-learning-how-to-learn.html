The idea

Learning improves when people retrieve ideas from memory, space practice over time, connect new concepts to prior knowledge and receive useful feedback. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

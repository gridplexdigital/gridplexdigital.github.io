Why humans make art

Art can express emotion, preserve memory, challenge norms, create shared identity or invite attention to form. It need not justify itself through practical utility.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Art can express emotion, preserve memory, challenge norms, create shared identity or invite attention to form. It need not justify itself through practical utility. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Spend time with a work before searching for its explanation. Notice what it makes you attend to, feel or question.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What artwork has changed how you notice something ordinary?

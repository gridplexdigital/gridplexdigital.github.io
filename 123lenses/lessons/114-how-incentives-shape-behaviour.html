The idea

People respond not only to values and instructions but also to rewards, penalties, status and the costs of choosing differently. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When evaluating a policy or workplace process, ask what behaviour it rewards in practice—not just what it says it values.

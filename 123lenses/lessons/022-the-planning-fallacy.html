The planning fallacy

The idea

People often underestimate the time, cost and difficulty of their own plans, even when similar projects have run late before. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Use the outside view: compare the plan with the actual history of similar work, then add contingency.

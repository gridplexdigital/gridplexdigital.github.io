Why intellectual humility improves judgment

The idea

Intellectual humility means recognising the limits of one's knowledge and remaining open to correction. It is compatible with firm conclusions when evidence is strong. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

State what you know, what you infer and what you do not know. Match confidence to the quality of evidence.

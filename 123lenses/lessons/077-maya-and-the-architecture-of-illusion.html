Maya: piercing the veil of sensory conditioning

The world as perceived through uncalibrated senses is a projection of conditioning. Mistaking the transient surface for permanent truth is the root of suffering.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Maya is not that the physical world does not exist; it is the illusion of permanence, separateness, and self-sufficiency. We treat temporary roles, wealth, and physical bodies as permanent absolutes, and suffer when they inevitably change. Recognizing Maya allows us to engage in worldly duties with playfulness, devotion, and perspective.

Put it to use

Remember that current corporate crises, political scandals, and personal dramas are transient waves on an ancient, deep sea.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What temporary problem are you currently treating as an absolute, permanent catastrophe?

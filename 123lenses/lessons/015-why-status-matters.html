The idea

Status influences attention, access, credibility and group behaviour. It can be earned through competence, generosity, courage, wealth, lineage or control of scarce resources. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Notice which forms of status a group rewards. Do not assume the loudest or most visible person has the most useful expertise.

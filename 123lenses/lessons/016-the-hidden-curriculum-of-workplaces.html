The hidden curriculum of workplaces

People learn workplace norms from what leaders reward, tolerate and model—not only from formal values statements.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

People learn workplace norms from what leaders reward, tolerate and model—not only from formal values statements. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Observe who gets promoted, whose mistakes are forgiven and which behaviours are quietly discouraged. These patterns reveal the operating culture.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What does an organisation teach through its actions rather than its slogans?

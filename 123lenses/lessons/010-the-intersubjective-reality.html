The intersubjective reality

Human cooperation at scale relies on intersubjective realities—shared fictions like money, corporations, and laws that exist only in our collective imagination.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Unlike objective realities (rocks, rivers) or subjective feelings (private pain, fear), intersubjective realities exist in the shared communication network between thousands or millions of minds. If one person ceases to believe, the reality remains; if everyone ceases to believe, it vanishes overnight. Treat institutional constructs as powerful coordination protocols, not physical laws of the universe.

Put it to use

When analyzing institutional gridlock or corporate conflicts, ask which shared stories everyone is treating as physical facts.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which shared fiction in your career or community exerts the greatest control over your daily decisions?

Identity: why belonging can override evidence

Group identity can shape what people notice, trust and defend. Criticism of an idea may be experienced as criticism of the group itself.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Group identity can shape what people notice, trust and defend. Criticism of an idea may be experienced as criticism of the group itself. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Separate the truth of a claim from whether it benefits your group. Apply the same evidentiary standard to allies and opponents.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

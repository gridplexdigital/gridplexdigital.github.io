The idea

Long-run prosperity is associated with interacting factors such as institutions, human capital, technology, geography, trade and state capacity. No single explanation fits every country. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Compare countries over time and use multiple indicators. Avoid treating one book's thesis as a complete theory of development.

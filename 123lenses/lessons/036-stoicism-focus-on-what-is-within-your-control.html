Stoicism: focus on what is within your control

Stoic practice distinguishes what depends on us—such as our judgments and actions—from what does not fully depend on us, such as reputation or other people's choices.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Stoic practice distinguishes what depends on us—such as our judgments and actions—from what does not fully depend on us, such as reputation or other people's choices. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Put effort into preparation, conduct and response. Do not confuse acceptance of limits with passivity about preventable problems.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What are you spending energy trying to control that is not yours to command?

How to think in probabilities

Many real-world judgments are uncertain rather than simply true or false. Probability helps express degrees of confidence and update beliefs as evidence changes.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Many real-world judgments are uncertain rather than simply true or false. Probability helps express degrees of confidence and update beliefs as evidence changes. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Use ranges and confidence levels. Record predictions and revisit them to calibrate your judgment.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What are you highly confident about but have never tried to estimate probabilistically?

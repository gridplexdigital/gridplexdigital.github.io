The sunk-cost trap

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Past expenditure cannot be recovered by continuing. The relevant question is whether the next unit of time, money or effort is worth investing. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Make the next decision as if you were choosing today with the information you now have. Treat past cost as history, not an obligation.

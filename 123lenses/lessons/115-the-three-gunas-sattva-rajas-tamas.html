The Three Gunas: the ecology of human motivation

All nature and human consciousness operate through three fundamental forces: clarity and harmony (sattva), passion and agitation (rajas), and inertia and darkness (tamas).

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Human psychological states are dynamic mixtures of the Gunas. Tamas manifests as procrastination, fog, and denial; Rajas manifests as frantic ambition, restless craving, and burnout; Sattva manifests as calm focus, wisdom, and selfless service. Mastery consists of recognizing which Guna is dominating your mind and applying conscious remedies.

Put it to use

When feeling restless and stressed (Rajas), slow down and cultivate calm clarity (Sattva); when feeling lazy and depressed (Tamas), introduce vigorous physical movement.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which of the three Gunas has dominated your mental state over the past forty-eight hours?

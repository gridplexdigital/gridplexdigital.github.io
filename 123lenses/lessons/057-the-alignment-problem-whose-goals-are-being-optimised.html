The alignment problem: whose goals are being optimised?

A system can satisfy a measurable objective while failing the broader human purpose behind it. Alignment requires attention to values, incentives, context and unintended effects.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

A system can satisfy a measurable objective while failing the broader human purpose behind it. Alignment requires attention to values, incentives, context and unintended effects. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

For any automated process, identify the stated objective, proxy metric, affected people and ways the system could succeed on paper while causing harm.

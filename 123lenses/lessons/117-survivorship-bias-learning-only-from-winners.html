Survivorship bias: learning only from winners

Studying only visible successes hides the failures that disappeared from view. This can make a strategy look more reliable than it is.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Studying only visible successes hides the failures that disappeared from view. This can make a strategy look more reliable than it is. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Ask what the comparison group includes, who dropped out and how many attempts failed before the visible success.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which success story would look different if you could see all the failures?

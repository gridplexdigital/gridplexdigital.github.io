Why first impressions can mislead

Early information can anchor later interpretation. First impressions are useful hypotheses, not reliable verdicts.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Early information can anchor later interpretation. First impressions are useful hypotheses, not reliable verdicts. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Keep an explicit distinction between what you observed and what you inferred. Update when new evidence arrives.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What have you inferred about someone that you have not actually observed?

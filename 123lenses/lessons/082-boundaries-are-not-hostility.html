Boundaries are not hostility

Boundaries clarify what you can accept, provide or participate in. They are most useful when communicated clearly and enforced consistently rather than used as threats.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Boundaries clarify what you can accept, provide or participate in. They are most useful when communicated clearly and enforced consistently rather than used as threats. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

State the limit, explain the practical consequence and avoid negotiating against yourself in the moment.

Why technology does not automatically create progress

Tools expand what people can do, but outcomes depend on institutions, skills, access, incentives and governance. Adoption is not the same as beneficial use.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Tools expand what people can do, but outcomes depend on institutions, skills, access, incentives and governance. Adoption is not the same as beneficial use. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

For any technology project, define the user problem, adoption conditions, failure modes and distribution of benefits.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Who gains, who bears the risks and who may be excluded by a new technology?

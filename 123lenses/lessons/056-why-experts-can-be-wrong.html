Why experts can be wrong

Expertise improves performance in domains with valid feedback and learnable patterns, but confidence can exceed evidence in uncertain or poorly calibrated environments.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Expertise improves performance in domains with valid feedback and learnable patterns, but confidence can exceed evidence in uncertain or poorly calibrated environments. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Ask how an expert knows, how often they receive feedback and whether the current problem resembles their area of competence.

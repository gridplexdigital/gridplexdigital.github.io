⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

A beginner's mind is a willingness to encounter familiar subjects without assuming that prior knowledge has settled every question. It makes room for observation and revision. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

When entering a new field, ask basic questions without embarrassment. Notice what experts may take for granted.

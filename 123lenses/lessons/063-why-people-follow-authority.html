Why people follow authority

Authority can coordinate action and transmit expertise, but status cues can also discourage scrutiny. A title is evidence of a role, not proof that every claim is correct.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Authority can coordinate action and transmit expertise, but status cues can also discourage scrutiny. A title is evidence of a role, not proof that every claim is correct. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Respect expertise while checking whether it is relevant to the question. Ask for reasons, evidence and uncertainty.

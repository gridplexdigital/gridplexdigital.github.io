The idea

A vivid individual story can distract from how common an outcome is in the relevant population. Base rates provide essential context. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Before accepting a prediction, ask: how often does this happen in comparable cases? Then consider the case-specific evidence.

How to become someone people trust with difficult problems

People rely on those who clarify ambiguity, communicate constraints, follow through and surface risks early. Expertise matters, but reliability makes it usable.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

People rely on those who clarify ambiguity, communicate constraints, follow through and surface risks early. Expertise matters, but reliability makes it usable. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Practise stating the problem, evidence, options, risks and next decision in a concise form. Do what you promise and flag delays early.

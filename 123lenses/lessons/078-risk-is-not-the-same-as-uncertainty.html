Risk is not the same as uncertainty

Risk can often be described with probabilities based on data or a defensible model. Uncertainty includes situations where probabilities or outcomes are poorly known.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Risk can often be described with probabilities based on data or a defensible model. Uncertainty includes situations where probabilities or outcomes are poorly known. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Ask whether you have reliable frequencies or merely a plausible story. Avoid false precision when the underlying uncertainty is deep.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Where are you treating an uncertain situation as if its probabilities were known?

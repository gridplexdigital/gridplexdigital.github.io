The tragedy of the commons—and its alternatives

Shared resources can be depleted when users face incentives to take more while costs are distributed. But collapse is not inevitable: communities can create workable governance rules.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Shared resources can be depleted when users face incentives to take more while costs are distributed. But collapse is not inevitable: communities can create workable governance rules. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Study real cases of successful common-resource management as well as failures. Pay attention to monitoring, participation and sanctions.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

What local resource could be managed better through collective rules?

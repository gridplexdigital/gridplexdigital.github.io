Rituals: why repeated actions matter

Rituals can create belonging, mark transitions, transmit values and coordinate groups. Their meaning may be social, emotional, religious or political.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

Rituals can create belonging, mark transitions, transmit values and coordinate groups. Their meaning may be social, emotional, religious or political. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Look at what a ritual makes visible: roles, shared commitments, hierarchy, memory and expectations.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Which ritual in your life creates meaning beyond its practical function?

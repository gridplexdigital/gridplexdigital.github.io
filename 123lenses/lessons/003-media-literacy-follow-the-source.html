Media literacy: follow the source

A claim can change as it travels through headlines, commentary and social media. Source tracing helps distinguish original evidence from repetition.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

A claim can change as it travels through headlines, commentary and social media. Source tracing helps distinguish original evidence from repetition. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Find the original report, dataset, speech or study. Check date, context, method and whether the claim is stronger than the source supports.

The idea

Compounding occurs when gains or interest are added to a base and future growth applies to the enlarged amount. It works in learning and relationships as well as finance. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Build small repeatable habits and give them time. Also recognise that fees, debt and risk can compound in the opposite direction.

The halo effect

A positive impression in one area can spill over into judgments about unrelated qualities. Confidence, charm or prestige can be mistaken for competence.

⚡ 2-Minute Field Note 📖 Looking for the full study? Read the 12-minute deep dive with book quotes →

The idea

A positive impression in one area can spill over into judgments about unrelated qualities. Confidence, charm or prestige can be mistaken for competence. Treat this as a lens for examining a situation, not as a universal explanation. Context matters: ask what evidence supports the interpretation, what it leaves out and when the idea might not apply.

Put it to use

Evaluate claims and track records separately. Ask what evidence supports each specific judgment.

A QUESTION TO CARRY

Whose confidence or reputation might be influencing your assessment?
